## A restaurant is two rooms at once

Guests see one room. The team works in another, laid over the top of it: the route from the pass to every table, the station where drinks are made, the corner where cutlery is reset between sittings. A restaurant interior has to be designed for both, because a beautiful dining room that slows the service down is a problem every night.

That is why we start with the plan and the numbers that matter to an operator, such as how many seats, how many sittings and how the queue behaves at the door, before choosing a single finish.

## Designed to be remembered

Once the room works, it has to be worth coming back to. The best restaurant interiors feel like they could only belong to that restaurant: its food, its story, its people. That comes from the brief, which is why we spend time with the owners before we draw.
