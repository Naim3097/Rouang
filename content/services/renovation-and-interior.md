## Planning comes first

Many spaces share the same problem: the rooms are the right size but the wrong shape for the people using them. A sofa that faces the wrong way, a kitchen that cannot fit two people cooking, a dining room that slows the service down.

So we begin with the plan. Once the layout works, the materials, lighting and joinery follow from it, and the budget goes into the things that are used every day rather than into fixing the plan later.

## Homes and businesses

In a home, that means planning around the way the household lives. In a restaurant, it means designing two rooms at once: the one guests see, and the one the team works in, from the route between the kitchen and every table to the place where the queue waits at the door.

In each case the approach balances design character, functionality and the practical requirements of the project.
