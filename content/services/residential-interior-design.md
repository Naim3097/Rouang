## Where a home project starts

Most homes we are asked to look at have the same problem: the rooms are the right size but the wrong shape for the people living in them. A sofa that faces the wrong way, a kitchen that cannot fit two people cooking, storage that is never where it is needed.

So we begin with the plan. Once the layout is right, the materials, lighting and joinery follow from it, and the budget goes into the things you will use every day rather than into fixing the plan later.

## Working to your budget

Good design is not one size fits all, and neither is the budget behind it. Tell us what you have to spend at the start. It changes the decisions we make, from which walls move to which finishes are worth the money, and it means the design you approve is one you can afford to build.
