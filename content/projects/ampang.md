## Inside

The house is white inside and out, and drawn in black: black-framed arched doors and windows, dark joinery with brass handles and wall lights. Arches repeat through the plan, from the front door to the timber-lined opening of the dressing room.

Pattern is kept for a few places where it counts: a black and white inlay on the foyer floor, a geometric lattice around the prayer niche, floral wallpaper in the powder room and a tufted red velvet seat set into an arched recess.
