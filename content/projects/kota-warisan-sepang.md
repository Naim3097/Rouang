concept: |
  The facade is treated as a composed whole: crisp white walls, recessed openings and a darker roofline, keeping the house's familiar pitched silhouette. Inside, arches, dark frames and wall panelling carry the exterior language through every room.
planning: |
  The shared rooms on the ground floor, living, dining, family hall and kitchen, are pale and open to the garden edge. A darker entertainment room is a deliberate change of mood. Upstairs, a private approach leads to the master suite, with a walk-in wardrobe built around an island and a bathroom arranged in clear wet and dry zones.
