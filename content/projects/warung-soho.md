standfirst: >-
  A proposed restaurant interior of fluted timber, terrazzo and cane, with a service counter set
  against red tile. Arched timber screens stand between the dining room and covered seating outside.
concept: >-
  The proposal sets fluted timber, terrazzo and cane against pale walls, and uses red at the
  counters: a tiled wall behind the service counter, red-lined shelves and the tiled end of the
  buffet. A large leaf-pattern mural in dark green, orange and cream runs along the wall behind the
  buffet.


  Green and cane carry through from the dining room to the terrace: green cane-backed chairs and
  hanging planting inside, a green steel frame and black cane-backed chairs outside. Arches repeat
  in the openings of the timber screens and in an arched opening beside the service counter.
planning: >-
  In the proposal, a buffet counter runs along the mural wall and the service counter sits across
  the far end of the room, with tables set out in rows. The frontage is a run of timber-framed
  screens with arched openings, a timber base, clear glass at eye level and reeded glass above, and
  the dining room is in view from the seating outside.


  The renders show covered seating outside on two sides of the frontage, one of them a terrace under
  a green steel frame with planting along its glazed street edge. The terrace floor is shown in
  terracotta-toned tiles, against the pale floor of the dining room.
lighting: >-
  In the renders, a concealed strip of light runs along the head of the pale wall above the service
  counter, below a dark ceiling, and the red-lined shelves are lit from within. Brass-toned wall
  lights with curved arms and slim glass shades sit on the mural wall and beside the counter, and
  clear glass globes hang among the planting over the tables.


  On the terrace, rattan pendants of different shapes hang in groups from the ceiling. Dappled
  daylight is shown on the timber screens and on the floor.
